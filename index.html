Compliance built in

Tax rules change.
Your software keeps up.

Both our home markets are moving to real-time, government-connected invoicing. We track the regulations so your finance team doesn't have to rebuild processes every budget season.

UAE e-invoicing go-live
—days to 1 Jan 2027

Businesses with AED 50M+ revenue must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 30 Oct 2026. Everyone else follows on 1 Jul 2027.

PKPakistan

FBR · PRA · SRB · EOBI
  • FBR digital invoicing

    Invoices go to FBR in real time through a licensed integrator or PRAL, as required under Sales Tax Rule 150Q, and print with the IRN and QR code.

  • Sales tax and withholding

    Annexure-ready sales tax returns, provincial sales tax on services (PRA, SRB, KPRA) and withholding tax statements, straight from your ledger.

  • Payroll deductions

    Salary income tax slabs updated each Finance Act, plus EOBI and provincial social security (PESSI, SESSI) contributions per employee.

AEUnited Arab Emirates

FTA · MoHRE · MoF
  • E-invoicing (PINT AE)

    Structured invoices in the Peppol PINT AE format, exchanged through your chosen Accredited Service Provider, ready for the 2027 mandate.

  • VAT and corporate tax

    5% VAT with reverse charge and FTA-format VAT 201 returns, plus the records and adjustments you need for 9% corporate tax filing.

  • WPS payroll and gratuity

    Salary Information Files (SIF) generated in the exact MoHRE and Central Bank format, with end-of-service gratuity accrued monthly.

Regulatory references current as of September 2026. We publish every rule change to clients before it takes effect.
